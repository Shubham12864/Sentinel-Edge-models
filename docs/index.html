FAQ

Questions everyone asks

Does my video leave my machine?

No. All inference runs locally on your CPU/GPU. Nothing is uploaded — the website is just where you download the app from.

What hardware do I need?

Any modern laptop works (CPU mode, ~1–3 fps/camera). An NVIDIA GPU unlocks full-speed multi-camera recognition. The setup wizard tells you exactly what your machine can do.

Is it really free?

Yes — open source under MIT (model weights AGPL-3.0). No accounts, no cloud subscription, no telemetry.
